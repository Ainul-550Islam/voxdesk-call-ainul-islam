P1-04 Legal Contract Intelligence • Playbooks & Redlines

Legal Agent — Clause Library, Playbooks, Diff Redlines & Audit Chain

Powered by app/legal/clause_engine.py, review_engine.py, playbook.py, redline.py, and clause_library.py with SHA-256 artifact fingerprinting and mandatory human review gates.

1. Clause Library & Risk Tiers

  • 5 canonical clause categories: termination, limitation_of_liability, indemnification, confidentiality, and data_protection.
  • Each entry includes risk_tier, version, pattern matcher, suggested_text, fallback_text, and SHA-256 fingerprint.
  • Searchable via GET /api/legal/clause-library?category=&q=.

2. Contract Playbooks & Rule Evaluation

  • Tenant- and environment-scoped playbooks with draft, active, and archived lifecycle states.
  • Evaluate contract findings against playbook rules (contains, equals, gte, lte) with severity and required actions.
  • Backed by PlaybookService and governance policy storage.

3. HTML Diff Redlines & Exportable Audit Packages

  • RedlineEngine generates side-by-side HTML diffs via difflib.HtmlDiff with rationale and evidence references.
  • Every artifact carries an explicit human-review disclaimer and pending/approved/rejected review_state.
  • Export complete cryptographic audit packages via POST /api/legal/redlines/export.
