Enterprise Quality Management System (QMS) • Native Adapters

Quality Management & Audit Readiness — Veeva Vault, MasterControl & ETQ

Backed by app/compliance/qms.py and app/compliance/qms_adapters.py with configured-control evaluation, CAPA/deviation traceability graphs, and audit package assembly.

1. Native QMS Adapters (3 Providers)

  • VeevaVaultAdapter: Session authentication (/api/v24.1/auth), controlled document queries, and exponential retry backoff.
  • MasterControlAdapter: Document lifecycle, CAPA findings, and control evidence retrieval.
  • ETQAdapter: ETQ Reliance quality events, deviations, and corrective action tracking.

2. Honest Health & Controlled Documents

  • POST /api/compliance/qms/health verifies live connectivity and reports honest unavailable status when credentials are not configured.
  • GET /api/compliance/qms/{provider}/documents lists controlled SOPs with revision, owner, effective_date, and approval_state.
  • Zero fabricated compliance rows or fake certifications.

3. CAPA Traceability & Audit Package Assembly

  • GET /api/compliance/qms/{provider}/traceability maps subjects to linked CAPAs, deviations, and control evidence.
  • POST /api/compliance/qms/{provider}/audit-package assembles controlled documents, findings, and reviewer sign-offs.
  • Full tenant, organization, and environment isolation on every QMS operation.
