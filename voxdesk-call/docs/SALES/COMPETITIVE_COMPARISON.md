# VoxDesk vs. Retell AI — Sourced Competitive Comparison (`2026-10-08`)

**Verification & Sourcing Policy (`R11` / Gate `G9`)**:
- Every VoxDesk capability claim in this document maps directly to one of the 41
  numbered rows in [`docs/SALES/FEATURE_MATRIX_VERIFIED.md`](FEATURE_MATRIX_VERIFIED.md)
  and its passing JUnit test evidence in `evidence/junit/feature_evidence.xml`.
- Every Retell AI description is explicitly labelled as a **Vendor claim** sourced
  from public Retell AI documentation (`https://docs.retellai.com` and
  `https://www.retellai.com`, accessed `2026-10-08`).

  `#19`, `#35`) are disclosed transparently and never marketed as `LIVE`.
